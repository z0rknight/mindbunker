03 · Colour tokens

Neutral structure, a little violet, rare semantic colour

Contrast values are measured against the stated surface. Zinc-500 (#71717A) fails AA on surfaces 2–3 and is replaced by muted #8C8C96.

Typical screen budget (of visible non-text area)

≈92% neutrals · ≈6% violet · ≈2% semantic (operator can reach ≈4–5% semantic on a War Room with live signals; client and public stay ≤1%).
